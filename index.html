Theory of change

How we move from insight to impact

Each step is a lever. Click to expand.

Step 01

Listen & learn

We begin with deep listening — to communities, to data, to lived experience. Our work is shaped by those we serve.

▾
Step 02

Co-design

We build programmes with, not for. Co-creation ensures relevance, ownership, and sustainability from day one.

▾
Step 03

Test & iterate

We pilot, measure, and adapt. Evidence-led iteration turns good intentions into proven practice.

▾
Step 04

Scale & share

What works, we scale. What we learn, we share — openly, so others can build on it.

▾

Who steers the foundation

Meet the team. Click to read more about each role.

Mandela Onwusah

Founder & Director

Mandela brings two decades of experience across development, policy, and social innovation.

He founded the IJIDI ecosystem to create a new kind of institutional architecture — one rooted in trust, transparency, and African agency. He is the author of several frameworks on civic infrastructure and participatory governance.

mandelaonwusah.com →

Ifeoma Peace David

Trustee

Ifeoma is a seasoned legal and policy advisor with deep expertise in human rights and institutional governance.

She has advised governments and civil society organisations on constitutional reform, access to justice, and institutional accountability. She is committed to ensuring the foundation operates with integrity and transparency.
